Read this first: one trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the only trophy the published guides flag as possibly missable. In Chapter 1, one morning after Lucia is home, she walks out to the balcony at Jason’s house to meditate — follow her and take the optional meditation. It is gone after Chapter 1, and whether it counts toward the 18 spots is still unconfirmed, so do it and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops. Everything else can be earned after the story, in free roam. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies — keep them off your trophy save.

Sources: GTABase, PowerPyx (via Output Lag), KeenGamer, Push Square. See each trophy’s own sources below.

Roadmap to the platinum

Play in this order and almost nothing needs a second run. Stages 1–2 follow the story; stages 3–5 are the open-world grind, best done while you travel; stage 6 is the cleanup. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, needs every other trophy, and the gold A Long Road needs 100% completion — the in-game checklist (Touch Pad > Progress > 100% Completion, after the story) is your final authority.
